Why Theory

Most brand video gets scrolled past. Ours gets finished.

The problem

Founders hire for a polished video, then watch 70 percent of viewers leave in the first eight seconds. The film looked fine. Nobody had decided who it was for or what they should do next.

The fix

We write the viewer's reason to stay before anyone picks up a camera. Every film has one audience, one idea, and one next step, and we measure each against what it was built to do.

Process

From first call to published film in three steps

You review the first cut, request changes, and receive final files for every channel along with a launch plan.

Questions

Answers before you book

What kind of video do you make?

Brand films, product explainers, founder interviews, and short cuts for social channels. Every project is planned around one audience and one action.

How long does a project take?

You see a first cut 11 days after kickoff. Final files usually arrive within three weeks, depending on how quickly feedback comes back.

What does it cost?

Pricing depends on scope. After the strategy call you receive a fixed quote, and the price does not change once you approve it.

Do you work with smaller brands?

Yes. Several clients started with a single short film and a team of three. We scope the work to match your stage.

Who owns the footage?

You do. Final films and raw footage are yours to use on any channel, with no usage fees or expiry dates.

What if I do not like the first cut?

Two rounds of revisions are included. If the film still misses the brief, we keep working on it until it matches what you approved.
